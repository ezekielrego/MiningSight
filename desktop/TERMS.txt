TunnelSight Desktop — Ezekiel Rego

TunnelSight Desktop connects to the existing TunnelSight service. Your projects,
files, permissions and account remain managed by that service. The desktop app
does not make cloud models, reconstruction APIs or cloud storage available offline.

Local screen capture begins only when you choose a window and start streaming.
Your selected window's video is transmitted to the TunnelSight relay associated
with the email you supply. The email is a destination label, not authentication.
Sign in through your browser to access your private projects in the workstation.
Choose only windows you intend to share. Phone control remains in the local
scrcpy mirror. No unattended remote control service is installed.

You may stop streaming at any time. Closing TunnelSight stops its own mirror and
encoder processes. Uninstall through Windows Settings > Apps. Uninstalling the
desktop app does not delete server projects or files. Local application settings
are retained for reinstalls; use Clear local data in Help before uninstalling to
remove them. Your Android USB-debugging authorization is managed on the phone.

The software is provided as available, without a guarantee of uninterrupted
network or device compatibility. Survey accuracy requires genuine calibrated
survey data. Review the service's About, Terms and Privacy pages for its data
processing practices, including software and model improvements.
