# MiningSight Privacy & Data Use

Effective date: 8 November 2026

## Information that may be processed

Depending on the features used, MiningSight may process Google account identity, project names, uploaded files, model and media metadata, annotations, measurements, keyframes, provider preferences, prompts, and generated results.

## How information is used

Data may be used to provide sign-in, private project storage, file validation, rendering, annotation history, AI analysis, reconstruction requests, error reporting, and service security. Project data and interaction information may also be used to improve the MiningSight software, user experience, reliability, and—where enabled by applicable provider settings and provider terms—to improve AI models or related services.

## Third-party services

Google may process sign-in information. Gemini, World Labs Marble, GOMERAX, hosting, storage, and other configured services may process data sent through features selected by the user. Users should review each provider’s privacy and data-use terms before submitting sensitive or restricted material.

## User choices

Users choose whether to sign in, upload files, configure a provider, or send material for AI processing. Users can avoid submitting sensitive information, remove project assets where the interface provides that option, and contact the project author about data questions or removal requests.

Do not upload information that you are not authorised to share. This project notice is informational and is not a substitute for jurisdiction-specific legal advice or a provider’s official privacy policy.
