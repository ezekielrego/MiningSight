Get started

From download to workspace.

Use a Windows 10 or 11 computer with an x64 processor. A keyboard, mouse, and a stable internet connection make the full workspace easier to use.

1

Install on your PC

Download TunnelSight-Setup.exe above, open it from your Downloads folder, and follow the installer prompts.

This Windows installer does not run on Android, iPhone, iPad, or macOS.

2

Open & sign in

Launch TunnelSight from the Start menu or its installed shortcut. Choose browser sign-in, sign in with the same Google account you use on the website, and approve the secure desktop sign-in request in your browser.

Return to the app. It opens the hosted workspace; your online projects stay attached to that account.

3

Link your stream

Confirm that the linked desktop account email matches your signed-in TunnelSight account.

Choose a source window, then select Start stream. Open your project’s live viewer to review the feed.

Choose your source

A local window. Or your phone.

Window capture and Android USB mirroring are local desktop tools. Cloud sign-in, online projects, uploads, and viewing a published stream still need internet access.

Stream any available window

  1. Open the application you want to capture on your Windows computer. Keep its window visible and unminimized.
  2. In TunnelSight’s desktop streaming controls, open the window picker and choose the window you want to share.
  3. Check the selected source, link using your account email, then select Start stream.
  4. Open the live viewer in your TunnelSight project. Stop the stream in the desktop controls when you finish.

Mirror an Android phone over USB

  1. On your Android phone, enable Developer options, then USB debugging. The location varies by phone.
  2. Connect it to your PC with a USB cable that supports data. Unlock the phone and accept the Allow USB debugging RSA authorization prompt for your computer.
  3. Use the desktop app’s Android mirroring controls to open the phone’s screen with scrcpy.
  4. Choose the resulting mirror window in the window picker, link with your TunnelSight email, and select Start stream.

Before sharing: check the selected window and close private content inside it. Android mirroring depends on the device allowing USB debugging and screen capture; some apps protect their video from capture.

Installed app help

Get back to work.

The app opens, but the workspace is blank or offline

Check your internet connection, then open the web workspace in a browser on the same computer. If the website loads, close and reopen the desktop app. Your account’s cloud projects require the hosted service to be reachable.

My projects or live stream are missing

Confirm you signed in with the same Google account used on the website. For streaming, the linked email must match that account’s email. Open the intended project’s live viewer, check that the source window is still available, and confirm the desktop stream has started.

My Android phone is not detected, or says unauthorized

Unlock the phone, enable USB debugging, and accept the computer’s RSA authorization prompt. Try a data-capable USB cable or another USB port. If the prompt does not appear, reconnect the phone; you can revoke USB debugging authorizations in Developer options and authorize this computer again. Some Windows devices also require the phone manufacturer’s USB driver.

The stream is black, frozen, or showing the wrong window

Bring the source window into view and restore it if minimized. Reopen the native window picker and select the intended window again, then restart the stream. For Android, confirm the scrcpy mirror is running and the phone is unlocked. Protected content may not allow capture.

Windows shows a download or installation warning

The installer is unsigned, so Windows may show “Unknown publisher” or a SmartScreen warning. Confirm that you downloaded it from this TunnelSight website, compare its SHA-256 checksum, and check the file with your Windows security tools. If you cannot verify the download or your organization blocks installation, use the browser workspace and contact your administrator.

How do I update or uninstall?

Close TunnelSight before running a newer installer from this page. To remove it, open Windows Settings → Apps → Installed apps (Windows 11), or Apps & features (Windows 10), select TunnelSight, and choose Uninstall. The uninstaller keeps local sign-in data and settings by default; select its optional removal checkbox to delete that local data too. Your online projects remain associated with your online account.

Can I keep using the website?

Yes. The web version remains available with its existing behavior. The desktop app adds local integration around the hosted workspace. On a phone or tablet, you can dismiss the desktop recommendation with “Continue in browser” to access settings and try the web tools.

Reading this on your phone?

Open this website on your Windows computer, then visit Desktop setup to download the installer. You can also keep using the browser workspace.
